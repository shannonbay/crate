Your music, your notes, your covers.

Crate is a music player for Windows built around albums and artists, the way you think about your music. Tag it, annotate it, and give it the covers it deserves.

See what it does Download

Albums first

Every release is an album, EP or single, worked out from its length and number of songs, and you can change it. Filter by type, and see an artist's albums, EPs and singles grouped on their page.

  • Album pages with your own description
  • Swap in any cover you like, or give a single song its own (great for singles released before the album)
  • Drag songs to reorder an album; the new track numbers are saved to the files

Fix a whole album's tags at once

Select songs with Ctrl- or Shift-click and edit their tags together. Fields that differ are left alone unless you type in them, and you can number the songs 1, 2, 3… in one go.

  • Tags are written into the music files, so every other player sees them too
  • Rename an album or artist and your notes, covers and band members follow
  • Or edit one field in place: hover any detail in the Now playing panel and click the pencil

Everything is a right-click away

Click a song to select it, then press Play. Right-click for Play next, Add to queue, Add to playlist, song details and tag editing. With several songs selected, it all applies to every one of them.

  • Sort the song list by title, artist, album or length
  • Search as you type across titles, artists and albums
  • Keyboard friendly: arrows, Enter, Space, Ctrl+A

Artists, with the people behind them

Give artists a photo and a bio, and list band members with their roles and years. Each member gets their own page, even with no solo music, and links back to their bands.

Playlists that are quick to build

Search your library, tick the songs you want across as many searches as you like, and add them in one go. Drag to reorder, save the queue as a playlist, and repeats are allowed.

Make it yours

Choose a built-in skin, or make your own: pick the colours, fonts and corner roundness, and set any image as a background. Share skins as files with friends.

Everything else you'd expect, and a few things you wouldn't

  • Now playing panel The current song's cover large, with its details and your notes, and the queue underneath.
  • Plays what you have MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC/M4A, OGG and Opus, including phone voice recordings.
  • Media keys Your keyboard's play, pause and skip keys work, and Windows shows what's playing.
  • Picks up where you left off The queue, song and position come back when you reopen Crate.
  • Always up to date Crate watches your music folders and adds new music within seconds.
  • Shuffle and repeat Shuffle, repeat all or repeat one, and turning shuffle off puts the order back.
  • Backups built in A backup every day, plus one-click backup and restore of everything you've added.
  • Your layout Drag the panel edges to size things the way you like; Crate remembers.
  • Private by design No account and no cloud. Your library and notes stay on your computer.

Get Crate

Crate is free, for Windows 10 and 11.

Download for Windows All releases

On the release page, download the file ending in _x64-setup.exe and run it. It installs for your Windows account, so no administrator rights are needed. Crate is in early development and the installer isn't signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.

Crate is built with Tauri, Rust and React. Report a problem or suggest a feature.
